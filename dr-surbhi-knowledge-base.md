# Dr. Surbhi Patne — Knowledge Base (AI Receptionist)

## About
Dr. Surbhi Patne is an Obstetrician & Gynaecologist based in Bhilai, Chhattisgarh, India.
Qualifications: MBBS, MS (Obstetrics & Gynaecology), and DNB (Obstetrics & Gynaecology).
She provides comprehensive women's healthcare — from pregnancy and antenatal care to gynaecological and laparoscopic surgery — with a warm, patient-first, and listening-focused approach, and a strong commitment to continuous learning and improving every patient's experience.

## Specialisation
Obstetrics & Gynaecology — women's health, pregnancy care, and gynaecological surgery.

## Services (what patients can consult her for)

Consultations (OPD):
- Antenatal (pregnancy) and postnatal care
- Gynaecological check-ups and examinations
- Family planning counselling and procedures, including IUCD (copper-T) insertion
- Basic infertility evaluation and counselling
- Obstetric and gynaecological ultrasound interpretation
- Treatment of common gynaecological infections and disorders
- Adolescent health and puberty-related concerns
- Cervical cancer screening (colposcopy, VIA/VILI)
- PCOS / PCOD, endometriosis, and menstrual & hormonal health
- Menopausal health and transition support
- High-risk pregnancy care — gestational diabetes (GDM), pregnancy-induced hypertension (PIH), and thyroid disorders in pregnancy

Procedures & Surgery (OT):
- Normal, instrumental, and Caesarean (LSCS) deliveries
- Minor gynaecological procedures — D&C, cervical biopsy
- Major gynaecological surgery — hysterectomy, ovarian cystectomy, myomectomy
- Diagnostic and advanced laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery, including LAVH / TLH
- Management of obstetric emergencies — postpartum haemorrhage (PPH), eclampsia, ruptured ectopic pregnancy
- Family planning surgery — tubal ligation
- Post-operative care and management of surgical patients

She has extensive hands-on surgical experience, including many independent Caesarean sections and emergency obstetric interventions.
